Privacy policy

for the app “Lerntaler – Chores & Rewards” (iPhone, iPad) and this website · Last updated: 14 November 2026

This is a translation for convenience. The German version is authoritative.

In short: Without a family account, all data stays on your device. If you want to connect several devices, you use the family account, which is part of the “Lerntaler Plus” subscription: the family is then stored in your own iCloud with Apple and shared with the devices you connect. There is no server of my own and no separate sign-in; I have no access to this data and do not receive it. Photos are additionally end-to-end encrypted. Children do not have an account of their own. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics. I only receive data if you send me an email.

2. What Lerntaler is

Lerntaler is a family app: parents create tasks and rewards, children complete tasks and collect coins. Children have no account of their own, no email address and no contact features. The parent area is protected by a PIN. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Purchases (the “Lerntaler Plus” subscription) are only possible in the parent area behind the PIN.

3. Use without a family account

Without a family account, all data – family name, child profiles, tasks, coin entries, rewards, savings goals, photos and settings – is stored only on the device. No data is transferred to me. The app schedules reminders locally on the device.

Like all app data, this data is part of the device backup (iCloud backup or backup on a computer) if you use it in the iOS settings; Apple’s terms apply. The photo key and recovery code, however, are kept in the keychain of this device only and are not backed up.

I have no access to this data; I neither receive nor process it. The app stores it on your device because this is strictly necessary for the function you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

The app only uses the camera and photo library when you take or choose a photo yourself or scan a QR code to connect a device. The app only asks for this permission during setup by the parents or behind the parent PIN; the children’s area never asks for permissions. Face ID is optional and checked by the operating system; the app receives no biometric data. The microphone is not used.

4. Family account via iCloud (several devices, Lerntaler Plus)

If you want to use Lerntaler on several devices, you subscribe to “Lerntaler Plus” and can choose to connect the family to iCloud (parent area → Settings → Devices & account → “Connect with iCloud”). Then:

  • Where the data is stored: the family is stored in the private iCloud database (CloudKit) of the parent who connects it and uses that parent’s iCloud storage. From there it is shared via iCloud sharing with the Apple IDs of the devices you connect by QR code, for example the child’s device or the second parent’s device. Every device needs an Apple ID with iCloud and iCloud Drive for this, including the child’s device; a child account in Apple’s Family Sharing is enough.
  • No server of my own, no separate sign-in: there is no Lerntaler account, no password and no email address. The devices sync directly via iCloud. I have no access to this data and receive none of it.
  • Family data: family name, first names or nicknames and school year of the children, chosen avatars and appearance, tasks, schedules, coin entries, rewards, savings goals and reminders, as well as the device names for the device list. This data is stored as an event log so that all devices have the same state.
  • Photos: task, memory and avatar photos are optional. They are resized on the device, stored without location data and additionally end-to-end encrypted (AES-GCM) before upload. The app stores the photo key as an end-to-end encrypted iCloud field in the family, which only the family’s Apple IDs can read. If iCloud ever fails to provide the key, the recovery code unlocks the photos. I cannot view the photos. Task photos are deleted automatically after the period chosen by the parents (30 days by default), in iCloud too.
  • Subscription status: so that Plus applies to all connected devices, a parent device stores the subscription status confirmed by Apple (StoreKit) in the family in iCloud: product, term, whether it is a free trial and whether the subscription renews. Names and payment details are not included. There is no server that checks purchases.

iCloud. iCloud is an Apple service that you use with your Apple ID. The iCloud terms and conditions and Apple’s privacy policy apply; Apple is independently responsible for it. I do not receive the family account data and have no access to it. The app stores it on your devices and in your iCloud because this is necessary for the function you requested.

Legal bases. To the extent this processing is attributed to me as the provider of the app:

  • Your own data as a parent (devices, subscription status) is processed to perform the terms of use for Lerntaler and Lerntaler Plus (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Your children’s data (first name or nickname, school year, avatars, tasks, coins, rewards, savings goals, photos, children’s devices) is processed on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). There is no contract with the children themselves. The legitimate interest is to provide the service you chose, and your interest as parents in organising tasks and rewards in your family across several devices; it matches your children’s interest in being recognised for what they have done. The data stays within your family, is not analysed or passed on, and you can delete it at any time.

Children’s devices are set up and connected by parents only. When connecting to iCloud you confirm that you have parental responsibility for the children in this family or act with the consent of those who do.

Obligation to provide data. You are under no legal or contractual obligation to use a family account or provide data. Without a family account Lerntaler works on one device; only syncing several devices is then not possible. Photos are always optional.

How long the data stays in iCloud is up to you:

  • End of Lerntaler Plus: syncing rests. The data stays on the devices and in iCloud; changes wait and are passed on as soon as Plus is active again. I do not delete anything, as I have no access to the data.
  • “Delete family account” (Settings → Devices & account; only for the parent who created the family) immediately deletes the family including photos from their own iCloud. The other devices lose the connection; the data on the device stays.
  • “Leave family” (second parent): this device leaves the family; the family stays for the others and the data on the device stays.
  • “Disconnect this device” ends a device’s connection; the data stays on the device. Devices can also be removed in the device list under Devices & account.
  • Task photos are deleted after the chosen period (30 days by default), in iCloud too; memory and avatar photos when you delete them.
  • iOS settings: you can also delete the Lerntaler data in the iOS settings via the management of your iCloud storage.

5. Notifications

With a family account, parent devices receive short notices such as “New task to approve” or “New reward request”, and children’s devices receive notices such as “Task approved” or “Redemption confirmed”, if notifications are allowed. For this, iCloud informs the connected devices of new entries with a silent notification via Apple. The app then reads the news from iCloud itself and shows a notification on the device; the notices contain no children’s names. I receive no device token and no content, and no third-party push service is involved. iOS delivers silent notifications without guarantee, often with a delay. Notifications can be turned off at any time in the iOS settings.

Task reminders and the end of a timer are scheduled locally on the device; no data is transferred for them.

6. App Store and Apple

Lerntaler is distributed through Apple’s App Store. Apple is responsible for downloads, updates and ratings; Apple’s privacy policy applies. “Rate Lerntaler” only opens the App Store page. Apple provides me with anonymised download statistics and, if you have agreed to share with developers, crash reports. I cannot identify individual people from them.

Apple handles the purchase, payment, free trial, Family Sharing, cancellation and refunds of “Lerntaler Plus” under its own responsibility. The app checks on the device via StoreKit whether a subscription exists. From Apple I only receive aggregated sales figures, but no names, contact details or payment details.

7. Feedback by email

“Send feedback” (parent area → Settings → App) opens your own email app with a prepared message to me that contains the app version and iOS version; it is only sent when you tap Send yourself. If you write to me this way or directly by email, you send me your email address, the content and, where applicable, this information. Apart from Apple’s statistics (section 6), this is the only way I receive data. I use it only to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete it once the matter is closed and no retention obligation applies. Please do not send photos of your children by email.

9. No sharing

I do not sell data or use it for advertising. Lerntaler contains no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no third-party components (SDKs) that collect data. There is no automated decision-making and no profiling.

10. Your rights

In the parent area, parents can delete individual photos, reset content, export data, remove devices, leave the family and delete the family account completely at any time. Where I process personal data, for example your emails, you also have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). An email to hankanton.apps@gmx.de is enough. You manage the data in your iCloud yourself in the app and in the iOS settings; for iCloud itself, Apple is your point of contact.

Right to object. Where I process data on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) – this concerns feedback by email, the operation of this website and, to the extent attributed to me, the children’s data in the family account – you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. An email to hankanton.apps@gmx.de is enough.

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany. Users in Switzerland can contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

11. Explained for children

Lerntaler stores your name or nickname, your tasks, your coins and your wishes. Only your family and the devices your parents connected can see them. If several devices are connected, everything is kept in your parents’ iCloud. Nobody else can write to you here, and there are no ads. Photos are optional. They are encrypted so that only your family’s devices can show them. If there is something you don’t want or you have a question, tell your parents – they can change or delete everything.

If the app or the services involved change, I will update this policy. The version published here applies.
