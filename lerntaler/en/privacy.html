Privacy policy

for the app “Lerntaler: Chores & Rewards” (iPhone, iPad) and this website · Last updated: 6 November 2026

This is a translation for convenience. The German version is authoritative.

In short: Without a family account, all data stays on your device; I receive nothing. Only if you choose to create a family account to connect several devices is the family data stored on a server in the EU. Photos are encrypted beforehand and cannot be read by me. Children do not have an account of their own. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics. The family account is part of the “Lerntaler Plus” subscription, which is purchased through Apple.

2. What Lerntaler is

Lerntaler is a family app: parents create tasks and rewards, children complete tasks and collect coins. Children have no account of their own, no email address and no contact features. The parent area is protected by a PIN. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Purchases (the “Lerntaler Plus” subscription) are only possible in the parent area behind the PIN.

3. Use without a family account

Without a family account, all data – family name, child profiles, tasks, coin entries, rewards, savings goals, photos and settings – is stored only on the device. No data is transferred to me. The app schedules reminders locally on the device.

Like all app data, this data is part of the device backup (iCloud backup or backup on a computer) if you use it in the iOS settings; Apple’s terms apply. The session key, photo key and recovery code, however, are kept in the keychain of this device only and are not backed up.

I have no access to this data; I neither receive nor process it. The app stores it on your device because this is strictly necessary for the function you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

The app only uses the camera and photo library when you take or choose a photo yourself or scan a pairing code. Face ID is optional and checked by the operating system; the app receives no biometric data. The microphone is not used.

4. Family account (several devices, Lerntaler Plus)

If you want to use Lerntaler on several devices, you subscribe to “Lerntaler Plus” and can choose to create a family account. For this I process:

  • Subscription status: so that Plus applies to all of the family’s devices, the app reports the purchase’s transaction identifier to the server, and the server checks the subscription directly with Apple; Apple reports renewals, cancellations and refunds to the server directly (App Store Server Notifications). Product, term, status and Apple’s transaction identifier are stored – no names and no payment details.
  • Sign in with Apple: a pseudonymous Apple user identifier to recognise the parent account. The app does not request a name or email address from Apple, and I store neither. Apple is independently responsible for the sign-in itself.
  • Family data: family name, first names or nicknames and school year of the children, chosen avatars and appearance, tasks, schedules, coin entries, rewards, savings goals and reminders. This data is stored as an event log so that all devices have the same state.
  • Photos: task, memory and avatar photos are optional. They are resized on the device, stored without location data and end-to-end encrypted before transfer. The key is only on the family’s devices; I cannot view the photos. Task photos are deleted automatically after the period chosen by the parents (30 days by default).
  • Devices: a random device identifier, a device name, a session key and, if notifications are allowed, a push notification token. Sessions expire after 180 days without use.
  • Connection data: to protect against abuse (rate limiting, in particular during pairing) I do not store the IP address in plain text, only as a check value derived with a secret key, and for at most about two hours. Technical logs of the hosting provider are deleted automatically after a short time.

Where the data comes from. I do not receive your children’s data from the children themselves but from you as parents: you create the profiles, tasks and rewards. What children enter on paired devices (e.g. a completed task or a photo) also becomes part of the family account.

Legal bases.

  • Your own data as a parent (Apple user identifier, devices, subscription status): performance of the contract for Lerntaler and Lerntaler Plus (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Your children’s data (first name or nickname, school year, avatars, tasks, coins, rewards, savings goals, photos, children’s devices): legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). There is no contract with the children themselves. The legitimate interest is providing the service you chose and your interest as parents in organising tasks and rewards in your family across several devices; it matches your children’s interest in getting recognition for what they have done. To protect children: no ad banners, no tracking, no profiling, no account and no contact features for children, minimal data, encrypted photos and deletion by you at any time. I do not ask for consent from or for the children.
  • Subscription status: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); assigning a purchase to one family only also serves abuse prevention (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Push notifications: Art. 6(1)(b) for parent devices, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for children’s devices (as above).
  • Connection data and rate limiting: my legitimate interest in secure operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Children’s devices are set up and paired by parents only. When creating the family account you confirm that you have parental responsibility for the children or act with the consent of those who do.

Obligation to provide data. You are under no legal or contractual obligation to create a family account or provide data. Without a family account Lerntaler works on one device; only syncing several devices is then not possible. Photos are always optional.

Storage period.

  • While Lerntaler Plus is active, I store the family account data for as long as it exists.
  • End of Lerntaler Plus: if the subscription ends (including a grace period from Apple), the family account stays for another 90 days so that you can resume Plus without losing data; after that I delete all family account data including photos and subscription status automatically. The app shows the deletion date in the parent area. The data on your devices stays.
  • No use: if no device of the family has connected for twelve months and Plus is not active, I delete the family account automatically. Parent accounts without a family are deleted after twelve months without sign-in.
  • Deletion by you: “Delete family account” immediately removes all data including photos, devices, sign-ins and subscription status. “Delete my account” removes only your sign-in and your devices; the family account stays for the others. In both cases I also revoke the connection to the Apple ID with Apple.
  • Task photos are deleted after the chosen period (30 days by default), memory and avatar photos when you delete them.

5. Hosting

The family account is operated by Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (Cloudflare Workers, D1 and R2) as a processor, with family data and photos stored in the European Union. This is based on Cloudflare’s data processing agreement. As Cloudflare routes requests through a global network, transfers to the USA may occur; these are based on the European Commission’s adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under which Cloudflare is certified, and additionally on standard contractual clauses; for users in Switzerland the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework applies accordingly. Cloudflare processes requests at the nearest location of its network, so when travelling also outside the EU. The detailed guide is delivered by the same server; the information on connection data applies.

6. Push notifications

If notifications are allowed, parent devices receive short notices such as “New task to approve” or “New reward request”; children’s devices receive notices such as “Task approved” or “Redemption confirmed”, each with the name of the task or reward. The notices contain no children’s names. They are delivered via the Apple Push Notification service. For delivery I use the push service of Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA) as a processor; it processes the device token and the notification text and stores them only as long as needed for delivery. Under its terms Expo may also use data in aggregated or de-identified form for its own purposes (improving its services) and is a controller to that extent. Expo is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the transfer to the USA is based on this and on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses, for users in Switzerland on the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Notifications can be turned off at any time in the iOS settings; then no token is reported.

Task reminders are scheduled locally on the device; no data is transferred for them.

7. App Store and Apple

Lerntaler is distributed through Apple’s App Store. Apple is responsible for downloads, updates and ratings; Apple’s privacy policy applies. “Rate Lerntaler” only opens the App Store page. Apple provides me with anonymised download statistics and, if you have agreed to share with developers, crash reports. I cannot identify individual people from them.

Apple handles the purchase, payment, free trial, Family Sharing, cancellation and refunds of “Lerntaler Plus” under its own responsibility. From Apple I only receive the purchase confirmation (see section 4) and aggregated sales figures, but no names, contact details or payment details.

8. Feedback by email

If you write to me via “Send feedback” or directly by email, you send me your email address, the content and, with “Send feedback”, the attached app and operating system version. I use them only to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once the matter is closed and no retention obligation applies. Please do not send photos of your children by email.

10. No sharing

Data is not sold to third parties or used for advertising. Apart from the processors named above, nobody has access. There is no automated decision-making and no profiling.

11. Your rights

In the parent area, parents can delete individual photos, reset content, export data, remove devices, delete their own account and delete the family account completely at any time. You also have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). An email to hankanton.apps@gmx.de is enough.

Right to object. Where I process data on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) – in particular your children’s data and connection data – you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation; you exercise this right for your children as their parents. The easiest way is to delete the family account or individual content in the parent area; an email to hankanton.apps@gmx.de is also enough.

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany. Users in Switzerland can contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

12. Explained for children

Lerntaler stores your name or nickname, your tasks, your coins and your wishes. Only your family and the devices your parents connected can see them. Nobody else can write to you here, and there are no ads. Photos are optional. They are encrypted so that only your family’s devices can show them. If there is something you don’t want or you have a question, tell your parents – they can change or delete everything.

13. Changes

If the app or the services involved change, I will update this policy. The version published here applies.
