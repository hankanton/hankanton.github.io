Privacy policy

for the app “Lerntaler – Learn & Earn” (iPhone, iPad) and this website · Last updated: 6 November 2026

This is a translation for convenience. The German version is authoritative.

In short: Without a family account, all data stays on your device; I receive nothing. Only if you choose to create a family account to connect several devices is the family data stored on a server in the EU. Photos are encrypted beforehand and cannot be read by me. Children do not have an account of their own. There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no purchases.

2. What Lerntaler is

Lerntaler is a family app: parents create tasks and rewards, children complete tasks and collect coins. Children have no account of their own, no email address and no contact features. The parent area is protected by a PIN. There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no purchases.

3. Use without a family account

Without a family account, all data – family name, child profiles, tasks, coin entries, rewards, savings goals, photos and settings – is stored only on the device. No data is transferred to me. The app schedules reminders locally on the device.

The app only uses the camera and photo library when you take or choose a photo yourself or scan a pairing code. Face ID is optional and checked by the operating system; the app receives no biometric data. The microphone is not used.

4. Family account (several devices)

If you want to use Lerntaler on several devices, you can choose to create a family account. For this I process:

  • Sign in with Apple: a pseudonymous Apple user identifier to recognise the parent account. I do not store an email address. Apple is independently responsible for the sign-in itself.
  • Family data: family name, first names or nicknames and school year of the children, chosen avatars and appearance, tasks, schedules, coin entries, rewards, savings goals and reminders. This data is stored as an event log so that all devices have the same state.
  • Photos: task, memory and avatar photos are optional. They are resized on the device, stored without location data and end-to-end encrypted before transfer. The key is only on the family’s devices; I cannot view the photos. Task photos are deleted automatically after the period chosen by the parents (30 days by default).
  • Devices: a random device identifier, a device name, a session key and, if notifications are allowed, a push notification token.
  • Connection data: the IP address is stored for at most about two hours to protect against abuse (rate limiting, in particular during pairing). Technical logs of the hosting provider are deleted automatically after a short time.

The legal basis is the performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for abuse prevention, my legitimate interest in secure operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For children under 16, the parents act as holders of parental responsibility; children’s devices are set up and paired by parents only.

I store the family account data for as long as the family account exists. When it is deleted, I remove all data including photos without delay.

5. Hosting

The family account is operated by Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (Cloudflare Workers, D1 and R2) as a processor, with family data and photos stored in the European Union. This is based on Cloudflare’s data processing agreement. As Cloudflare routes requests through a global network, transfers to the USA may occur; these are based on the European Commission’s adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under which Cloudflare is certified, and additionally on standard contractual clauses. The detailed guide is delivered by the same server; the information on connection data applies.

6. Push notifications

If notifications are allowed, parent devices receive short notices such as “Lea handed in a task” or “Lea would like to redeem a reward”; children’s devices receive notices such as “Task approved” or “Redemption confirmed” with the name of the task or reward. They are delivered via the Apple Push Notification service. For delivery I use the push service of Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA) as a processor; it processes the device token and the notification text including the child’s first name and stores them only as long as needed for delivery. Expo is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the transfer to the USA is based on this and on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. Notifications can be turned off at any time in the iOS settings; then no token is reported.

Task reminders are scheduled locally on the device; no data is transferred for them.

7. App Store and Apple

Lerntaler is distributed through Apple’s App Store. Apple is responsible for downloads, updates and ratings; Apple’s privacy policy applies. “Rate Lerntaler” only opens the App Store page. Apple provides me with anonymised download statistics and, if you have agreed to share with developers, crash reports. I cannot identify individual people from them.

8. Feedback by email

If you write to me via “Send feedback” or directly by email, you send me your email address, the content and, with “Send feedback”, the attached app and operating system version. I use them only to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once the matter is closed and no retention obligation applies. Please do not send photos of your children by email.

9. This website

This website is provided via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, the date and time of access and the page requested, in order to deliver the page and protect the service against abuse. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in providing the website securely and reliably). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; transfers to the USA are based on this and on standard contractual clauses. Details are given in GitHub’s privacy statement.

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking services and loads no content from other servers (not even fonts).

10. No sharing

Data is not sold to third parties or used for advertising. Apart from the processors named above, nobody has access. There is no automated decision-making and no profiling.

11. Your rights

In the parent area, parents can delete individual photos, reset content, export data, remove devices and delete the family account completely at any time. You also have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). An email to hankanton.apps@gmx.de is enough. You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

12. Changes

If the app or the services involved change, I will update this policy. The version published here applies.
