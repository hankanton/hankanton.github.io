Terms of use

for the app “Lerntaler – Learn & Earn” · Last updated: 6 November 2026

This is a translation for convenience. The German version is authoritative.

1. What Lerntaler is for

Lerntaler is a free family app for parents and their children. Parents create tasks, rewards and savings goals and approve completed tasks; children collect coins (“Lerntaler”) for them. The coins are a motivation system within the family – not money, not a means of payment and not a voucher. The app does not arrange purchases and does not enter into contracts with children.

2. Parents’ responsibility

The family account is set up by a parent, who is responsible for it. Children’s devices are paired by parents only; the parent PIN and the recovery code must not be shared with children. Photos should only show the task; photos of other people require their consent.

3. Ongoing development

Lerntaler is continuously developed; features and content may change between versions. I recommend using “Export data” in the parent area regularly. Apple’s terms also apply to obtaining the app from the App Store.

4. Availability

Without a family account, the app works entirely on the device. The family account is operated on a best-effort basis; I cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability. All data is also kept on the connected devices and is synced automatically after an interruption.

5. Ending use

Parents can disconnect devices at any time and delete the family account completely in the parent area. The data on the devices can be kept if desired.

6. Contact

Questions and comments to hankanton.apps@gmx.de. Provider: see the legal notice. How data is processed is described in the privacy policy.
