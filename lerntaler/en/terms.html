Terms of use

for the app “Lerntaler: Chores & Rewards” · Last updated: 6 November 2026

This is a translation for convenience. The German version is authoritative.

1. What Lerntaler is for

Lerntaler is a family app for parents and their children. Parents create tasks, rewards and savings goals and approve completed tasks; children collect coins (“Lerntaler”) for them. The coins are a motivation system within the family – not money, not a means of payment and not a voucher. On one device Lerntaler is free. The app does not enter into contracts with children; purchases are only possible in the parent area behind the PIN.

2. Parents’ responsibility

The family account is set up by a parent, who is responsible for it. Children’s devices are paired by parents only; the parent PIN and the recovery code must not be shared with children. Photos should only show the task; photos of other people require their consent.

3. Lerntaler Plus

For several devices with a shared family account there is the “Lerntaler Plus” subscription, monthly or yearly, with a 14-day free trial and Family Sharing. It is purchased, paid and billed through Apple. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period in the Apple ID settings; any unused part of the free trial is forfeited when a subscription is purchased. Apple’s terms apply to the purchase, withdrawal and refunds; use of the app is governed by Apple’s Standard End User Licence Agreement (EULA) together with these terms. When the subscription ends, the devices continue to work with their data individually.

4. Ongoing development

Lerntaler is continuously developed; features and content may change between versions. I recommend using “Export data” in the parent area regularly. Apple’s terms also apply to obtaining the app from the App Store.

5. Availability

Without a family account, the app works entirely on the device. The family account (Lerntaler Plus) is operated on a best-effort basis; I cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability. All data is also kept on the connected devices and is synced automatically after an interruption.

6. Ending use

Parents can disconnect devices at any time and delete the family account completely in the parent area. The data on the devices can be kept if desired.

7. Contact

Questions and comments to hankanton.apps@gmx.de. Provider: see the legal notice. How data is processed is described in the privacy policy.
