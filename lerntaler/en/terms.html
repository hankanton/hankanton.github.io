Terms of use

for the app “Lerntaler: Chores & Rewards” · Last updated: 6 November 2026. These terms apply together with Apple’s Standard End User Licence Agreement (EULA) to the use of the app and the family account.

This is a translation for convenience. The German version is authoritative.

1. What Lerntaler is for

Lerntaler is a family app for parents and their children. Parents create tasks, rewards and savings goals and approve completed tasks; children collect coins (“Lerntaler”) for them. The coins are a motivation system within the family – not money, not a means of payment and not a voucher. Lerntaler is free to download and use; extra features come with the “Lerntaler Plus” subscription (section 3). The app does not enter into contracts with children; purchases are only possible in the parent area behind the PIN.

2. Parents’ responsibility

The family account is set up by a parent, who is responsible for it. Children’s devices are paired by parents only; the parent PIN and the recovery code must not be shared with children. Photos should only show the task; photos of other people require their consent.

3. Lerntaler Plus

Plus includes the family account for several devices (sync, notifications between devices, encrypted photos), the weekly review per child, study plans up to the test date, the family challenge and the looks “Night shift”, “Streak” and “Notebook”. Everything else, including the “Standard” look and all avatars, stays free. Plus is available monthly or yearly, with a 14-day free trial (once per Apple ID) and Family Sharing. Sale, payment, billing, withdrawal and refunds are handled by Apple (seller: Apple Distribution International Ltd.) under its terms. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period in the Apple ID settings. Any unused part of the free trial is forfeited when a subscription is purchased.

When Plus ends, the data on the devices stays. The children see the “Standard” look again; the chosen looks stay saved and apply again as soon as Plus is active. Existing study plans and challenges keep running; new ones can only be created with Plus, and new devices can no longer be paired. The family account stays for another 90 days so that the family can continue with a new subscription without losing data; after that its data is deleted from the server. The app shows the deletion date in the parent area (see the privacy policy).

4. Ongoing development and changes

I keep developing Lerntaler and fix bugs and security issues through app updates. Beyond that I only change features for a valid reason, for example new iOS versions, legal requirements, security, or because a feature is hardly used and maintaining it is disproportionate. This causes no additional costs for you. If a change restricts a Plus feature more than insignificantly, I will inform you in good time beforehand in the app and on this page; you can then cancel the subscription with Apple and request a refund for the unused period. I recommend using “Export data” in the parent area regularly.

5. Availability

Without a family account the app works entirely on the device. I operate the family account with due care at Cloudflare; the database and photos are stored in the EU. Short interruptions, for example for maintenance, are possible. All data is also kept on the connected devices and is synced automatically after an interruption. Your statutory rights in case of defects remain unaffected.

6. If I discontinue the family account

Should I ever discontinue the family account or Lerntaler Plus, I will announce it in the app at least twelve months in advance and stop offering Plus to new subscribers from the announcement. Until the end the family account keeps working. The app remains usable on the devices and the data can be exported. For periods already paid that can no longer be used you receive a pro-rata refund via Apple.

7. Content and reports

Only content that belongs to the family and its tasks may be stored in Lerntaler. Illegal content, such as photos of other people without their consent, is not allowed. Photos are end-to-end encrypted; I cannot view them. You can report illegal content at any time to hankanton.apps@gmx.de with the subject “Report”. Please say what it is about, why the content is illegal and how I can reach you. I confirm receipt, review the report and tell you the result. If I block or delete data or a family account, I give the family’s parents the reasons. Single point of contact for users and authorities under Articles 11 and 12 of the Digital Services Act: hankanton.apps@gmx.de (German, English).

For children: if anything in Lerntaler bothers or scares you, tell your parents.

8. Liability

I am liable without limitation for intent and gross negligence, for injury to life, body or health and under the German Product Liability Act. For slight negligence I am only liable if I breach an essential obligation on whose fulfilment you may rely, and only for the foreseeable, typically occurring damage. In that case I am liable for loss of data only to the extent that would also have occurred with regular backups (export). Your statutory rights in case of defects against Apple remain unaffected.

9. Ending use

Parents can disconnect devices at any time and delete the family account completely in the parent area. The data on the devices can be kept if desired. The licence to use the app is unlimited in time; you can end it at any time by deleting the app. You cancel a running Plus subscription with Apple – deleting the app does not end the subscription.

Questions and comments to hankanton.apps@gmx.de. Provider: see the legal notice. How data is processed is described in the privacy policy. I am neither obliged nor willing to take part in dispute resolution proceedings before a consumer arbitration board.
