Lerntaler: Chores & Rewards

Tasks and rewards for the whole family – on iPhone and iPad.

With Lerntaler, parents set up learning tasks and chores. Children see on their device what is due today, start tasks with a timer and hand them in. Parents approve, and the child earns coins (“Lerntaler”) for it. They can redeem them for small rewards or save up in a piggy bank for something big.

Free, no ad banners, no tracking. Lerntaler has no ad banners and no analytics. Without a family account, all data stays on the device. The family account for several devices and some extra features come with the “Lerntaler Plus” subscription; photos are encrypted before they are transferred. More in the privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

How do I set up Lerntaler?

  1. Open the app and create a family: family name, parent PIN (four digits) and the children with their school year.
  2. Write down the recovery code that is shown and keep it safe.
  3. In the parent area, go to Tasks and pick a template pack for the school year or create your own tasks.
  4. Under Rewards, add something to redeem right away and a piggy bank for a bigger goal.

Do I need an account?

No. On a single device Lerntaler works without any account. You only need a family account if several devices should share the same data, for example the parent’s iPhone and the child’s iPad; it is part of Lerntaler Plus. You sign in with “Sign in with Apple”; children do not have an account of their own.

How do I connect my child’s device?

On the parent device: parent area → Settings → Devices & account → “Sign in with Apple” (the family is then moved into the family account), then “Pair child device” and choose the child. On the child’s device, install Lerntaler, choose “Join a family” and scan the QR code. The QR code also transfers the key for photos. A second parent joins via “Invite second parent”.

I forgot the parent PIN.

Tap “Forgot PIN?” when entering the PIN and enter the recovery code you wrote down during setup. Then choose a new PIN.

How do rewards and piggy banks work?

The child requests a reward or puts coins into a piggy bank. Parents confirm the request; the child then sees “Confirmed”. After handing over the reward, parents tap “Redeemed together”, optionally with a photo for the album. When a savings goal is redeemed, there is a small goal bonus.

Are Lerntaler coins real money?

No. The coins are a motivation system within the family – not money, not a means of payment and not a voucher. You as parents decide what a reward costs and what it is.

What are looks?

Each child can choose a look under “Appearance” in their profile: “Standard”, “Night shift” (dark and calm), “Streak” (bright and sporty) or “Notebook” (paper and markers). A look changes colours, fonts, symbols and the start image, not the tasks or coins. You change the parent area’s look under Settings → App.

What happens to photos?

Photos are always optional. They are resized on the device and stored without location data. With a family account they are encrypted before transfer; the key is only on the family’s devices. Task photos are deleted after the period you set (30 days by default); memory photos in the album stay.

Notifications don’t arrive.

Allow notifications in the parent area under Settings → Reminders & notifications and in the iOS settings. Lerntaler also shows there whether the device is registered for push notifications. Push notifications between devices require a family account (Lerntaler Plus).

How do I back up or delete data?

  • Back up: Settings → Data & privacy → “Export data” or “Import data”.
  • Delete a child: Settings → Children → child → “Delete child”. For 30 days it can be restored under “Recently deleted”.
  • Start over: Settings → Data & privacy → “Reset everything” deletes tasks, rewards, piggy banks, coins and album; children, PIN and account stay.
  • Delete the family account: Settings → Devices & account → “Delete family account” removes all data from the server.
  • Delete your own account (second parent): Settings → Devices & account → “Delete my account” removes your own sign-in and devices; the family stays for the others.

What does Lerntaler cost?

Lerntaler is free to download and use: tasks, approvals, rewards, piggy banks, the “Standard” look and all avatars. The Lerntaler Plus subscription adds the family account for several devices, the weekly review, study plans, the family challenge and the looks “Night shift”, “Streak” and “Notebook”. It is monthly or yearly, with the first 14 days free (paid afterwards) and Family Sharing; the App Store shows the price in your currency. Subscribe, “Restore purchases” and “Redeem code”: parent area → Settings → Lerntaler Plus. Cancel: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Contact

Questions, bugs or wishes: the easiest way is in the app under Settings → App → “Send feedback”, which adds the app and iOS version. Or by email to hankanton.apps@gmx.de (English or German). It helps to mention the device, the steps and what you expected.
