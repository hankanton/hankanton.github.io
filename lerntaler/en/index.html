Lerntaler: Chores & Rewards

Tasks and rewards for the whole family – on iPhone and iPad.

With Lerntaler, parents set up learning tasks and chores. Children see on their device what is due today, start tasks with a timer and hand them in. Parents approve, and the child earns coins (“Lerntaler”) for it. They can redeem them for small rewards or save up in a piggy bank for something big.

Free, no ad banners, no tracking. Lerntaler has no ad banners and no analytics. Without a family account, all data stays on the device. The family account for several devices (via your own iCloud) and some extra features come with the “Lerntaler Plus” subscription; photos are additionally encrypted. More in the privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

How do I set up Lerntaler?

  1. Open the app and create a family: family name, parent PIN (four digits) and the children with their school year.
  2. Write down the recovery code that is shown and keep it safe.
  3. In the parent area, go to Tasks and pick a template pack for the school year or create your own tasks.
  4. Under Rewards, add something to redeem right away and a piggy bank for a bigger goal.

Do I need an account?

No. Lerntaler has no sign-in and no account of its own, not for children either. On a single device everything just works. If several devices should share the same data, for example the parent’s iPhone and the child’s iPad, you need Lerntaler Plus and iCloud: every device must be signed in to iCloud with an Apple ID and have iCloud Drive turned on, including the child’s device. A child account in Apple’s Family Sharing is enough.

How do I connect my child’s device?

On the parent device: parent area → Settings → Devices & account → tick the confirmation of parental responsibility → “Connect with iCloud” (the family is then moved into your iCloud). Then “Pair child device” and choose the child (or “All children” for a shared device); a QR code appears. On the child’s device, install Lerntaler, choose “This device belongs to an existing family” on the start screen and scan the QR code. The device gets the key for photos from iCloud. A second parent connects the same way via “Invite second parent”. Only show the QR codes to your own devices.

Notifications on the child’s device: Switch profile → Device settings (parent PIN) → “Allow notifications”. The child is never asked for permissions.

New iPhone or reinstalled Lerntaler?

The parent who created the family installs Lerntaler, chooses “This device belongs to an existing family” on the start screen and then “Load family from iCloud”. The iPhone must be signed in to iCloud with the same Apple ID. No QR code is needed, it also works without an active Plus subscription, and the key for photos comes from iCloud too. A child’s device or the second parent’s device is connected again as the first time: show the QR code on the parent device and scan it.

I forgot the parent PIN.

Tap “Forgot PIN?” when entering the PIN and enter the recovery code you wrote down during setup. Then choose a new PIN.

How do rewards and piggy banks work?

The child requests a reward or puts coins into a piggy bank. Parents confirm the request; the child then sees “Confirmed”. After handing over the reward, parents tap “Redeemed together”, optionally with a photo for the album. When a savings goal is redeemed, there is a small goal bonus.

Are Lerntaler coins real money?

No. The coins are a motivation system within the family – not money, not a means of payment and not a voucher. You as parents decide what a reward costs and what it is.

What are looks?

Each child can choose a look under “Appearance” in their profile: “Standard”, “Night shift” (dark and calm), “Streak” (bright and sporty) or “Notebook” (paper and markers). A look changes colours, fonts, symbols and the start image, not the tasks or coins. You change the parent area’s look under Settings → App.

What happens to photos?

Photos are always optional. They are resized on the device and stored without location data. With a family account they are additionally end-to-end encrypted before they are uploaded to iCloud; only your family’s devices can read them. Task photos are deleted after the period you set (30 days by default), in iCloud too; memory photos in the album stay.

Notifications arrive late or not at all.

Notifications between devices come with the family account (Lerntaler Plus). Check on every device:

  • Notifications allowed: on the parent device under Settings → Reminders & notifications, on the child’s device under Switch profile → Device settings → “Allow notifications”, and in the iOS settings under Notifications → Lerntaler.
  • Background App Refresh: turn on iOS Settings → General → Background App Refresh, and for Lerntaler there too. Without it, notifications only arrive once Lerntaler is opened. Lerntaler then shows a notice with “Open Settings”.
  • Low Power Mode: it turns off Background App Refresh automatically.

Even when everything is set up correctly, notifications often arrive with a delay, sometimes only after a few minutes and several at once. iCloud delivers them as “silent” notifications whose timing iOS decides itself (depending on battery, usage and network). When you open the app, everything is up to date straight away.

Where is our data stored?

Without a family account, only on the device. With a family account, also in the iCloud of the parent who connected the family (it uses their iCloud storage), shared with your connected devices; photos are additionally end-to-end encrypted. There is no Lerntaler server, and the developer has no access to your data. More in the privacy policy.

How do I back up or delete data?

  • Back up: Settings → Data & privacy → “Export data” or “Import data”.
  • Delete a child: Settings → Children → child → “Delete child”. For 30 days it can be restored under “Recently deleted”.
  • Start over: Settings → Data & privacy → “Reset everything” deletes tasks, rewards, piggy banks, coins and album on all connected devices; children, PIN and connection stay.
  • Delete the family account (only the parent who created the family): Settings → Devices & account → “Delete family account” immediately deletes the family including photos from your iCloud. The other devices lose the connection; the data on this device stays.
  • Leave the family (second parent): Settings → Devices & account → “Leave family”; the family stays for the others.
  • Disconnect a device: Settings → Devices & account → “Disconnect this device” ends the connection; the data stays on the device.
  • Data in iCloud: you can also delete the Lerntaler data in the iOS settings via the management of your iCloud storage.

What does Lerntaler cost?

Lerntaler is free to download and use: tasks, approvals, rewards, piggy banks, the “Standard” look and all avatars. The Lerntaler Plus subscription adds the family account for several devices, the weekly review, study plans, the family challenge and the looks “Night shift”, “Streak” and “Notebook”. It is monthly or yearly, with the first 14 days free (paid afterwards) and Family Sharing; the App Store shows the price in your currency. In the family account, Plus applies to all connected devices; the child and the second parent do not need a subscription of their own. Subscribe, “Restore purchases” and “Redeem code”: parent area → Settings → Lerntaler Plus. Cancel: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Contact
