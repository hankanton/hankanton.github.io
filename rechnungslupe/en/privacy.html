Privacy policy

for the “Rechnungslupe: E-Rechnung” app (iPhone, iPad, Mac) and this website · Last updated: 14 October 2026

This is a translation for convenience. In case of doubt, the German version applies.

In short: The app processes your invoices solely on your device. I receive no invoices, no invoice contents and no data about how you use the app. There is no account, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics service. The app itself makes no network connection. From version 1.1 there are two exceptions, which only apply if you use them: buying “Rechnungslupe Pro” through the App Store, and the optional syncing of your archive via your own iCloud. Both run through Apple services; I receive neither invoices nor data about you.

2. Processing in the app

Invoices you open

When you open an invoice (from Mail, the Files app, the Finder or via “Open invoice”), the app reads the file in order to display it and check it for calculation and format errors (plausibility check). This happens solely on your device. The file is not transmitted or changed. The app stores invoices only if you add them to the archive yourself (see below). Copies that the operating system places in the app’s storage when you open a file from another app are deleted by the app again.

PDF export, check report and package for your tax adviser

The PDF of the view, the check report and the ZIP package for your tax adviser (the latter two from version 1.1) are created on your device. You decide where to save, print or share them; the app does not transmit them of its own accord.

Archive (from version 1.1)

If you add an invoice with “Add to archive”, the app stores the unchanged original file and, for searching, filtering and exporting, some details from it (seller, invoice number, date, amounts, currency, VAT ID, IBAN), together with your note on the invoice. The archive is kept on your device in the app’s storage. You can delete individual invoices at any time; deleting the app deletes the archive on the device.

Syncing via iCloud (from version 1.1, optional)

Syncing is off until you turn it on in the archive. The app then stores the invoices in the archive (original file, file name, date added and note) in the private database of your iCloud account (CloudKit) in order to sync them between your devices signed in with the same Apple Account. Apple provides this storage under its own responsibility; Apple’s terms and privacy policy apply. I have no access to this data. So that changes reach your other devices quickly, Apple sends your devices silent push notifications; these are not displayed and contain no invoice contents.

When you turn syncing off, the app asks whether the copies in iCloud should be deleted; the invoices on the device are kept in any case. If you delete the app, the copies in iCloud remain. You can delete them in Settings under “Apple Account” → “iCloud” → “Manage Storage”.

GiroCode, discount reminder and Quick Look (from version 1.1)

The app creates the GiroCode for your banking app on your device from the invoice’s payment details. A discount reminder is set up as an operating-system notification after you have allowed notifications; it contains the invoice number, seller, amount and deadline and stays on your device. The Finder preview on the Mac (Quick Look) also creates the readable invoice on your Mac.

Settings stored on your device

The app stores on your device:

  • your display setting for whether the numbers of the invoice fields (BT numbers) are shown;
  • the number of invoices opened successfully and the app version in which you were last asked for a rating – without any invoice contents. This means the app asks whether you would like to rate it no earlier than after the third invoice and at most once per version;
  • from version 1.1: whether “Rechnungslupe Pro” is unlocked, whether iCloud syncing is on, and the technical sync state (invoice identifiers in the form of checksums, without contents).

For troubleshooting, the app writes brief entries about iCloud syncing to the device’s system log (shortened checksums and counts, no invoice contents or notes). The log does not leave the device of its own accord.

This information does not leave your device, except as part of your own device backups. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Rating prompt

The rating prompt is a dialogue provided by the operating system. Apple decides whether it appears. Any rating you give goes to Apple and is published on the App Store.

3. App Store and Apple

The app is distributed through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes data under its own responsibility for downloads, updates and purchases; Apple’s privacy policy applies. From Apple, I receive only aggregated figures, for example on downloads and sales, but no names or contact details of buyers.

From version 1.1 you can buy “Rechnungslupe Pro” as a one-off in-app purchase. Apple handles the purchase, payment, Family Sharing and restoring purchases. I receive no names and no payment details. The app asks the operating system whether Pro has been purchased and remembers the result on the device so that Pro also works offline.

If you have allowed analytics data to be shared with app developers in your device settings, Apple provides me with crash reports and aggregated usage statistics. These contain no invoice contents, and I cannot attribute them to any person. I use them only to fix bugs. You can turn sharing off at any time in Settings under “Privacy & Security” → “Analytics & Improvements”.

4. Contact by email

If you email me, I process your email address, your name (if given), the content of the message and any attached files in order to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to using the app, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries).

If you attach an invoice so that I can reproduce a display problem, it will often contain personal data, including data of third parties (for example names, addresses, bank details). Please only send invoices if you are entitled to do so, and where possible remove details that are not needed to investigate the problem. I use such files only to investigate the problem, do not pass them on, and delete them as soon as the problem has been fixed or the enquiry has been dealt with.

I delete emails once they are no longer needed to deal with your enquiry, unless statutory retention obligations prevent this. Emails are stored with the email provider GMX (1&1 Mail & Media GmbH, Germany).

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21).
