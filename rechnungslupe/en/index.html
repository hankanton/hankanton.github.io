Rechnungslupe: E-Rechnung

Read e-invoices with ease – on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Rechnungslupe displays electronic invoices in the XRechnung, ZUGFeRD and Factur-X formats in a clear layout: seller and buyer, invoice lines, VAT, totals, payment details and attachments. You can save, print and share the view as a PDF.

Offline. Invoices are processed solely on your device. The app makes no network connection to do so, needs no account and contains no advertising, no tracking and no usage analytics. See the privacy policy for details.

Help

How do I open an invoice from Mail?

Tap the attachment (XML or PDF file). In the share sheet, choose either the “Rechnungslupe” app or, further down, the “View in Rechnungslupe” action. The action shows the invoice right inside Mail without switching apps.

How do I open an invoice from the Files app?

Touch and hold the file, then choose “Share” or “Open With” and “Rechnungslupe”. Alternatively, open Rechnungslupe and tap “Open invoice”. On a Mac, use File → Open…, right-click and choose “Open With”, or drag the file onto the app icon.

Which formats are supported?

  • XRechnung in both syntaxes (UBL and UN/CEFACT CII) as an XML file
  • ZUGFeRD 2.x and Factur-X: PDF files with an embedded XML invoice. With the MINIMUM and BASIC WL profiles, the file contains only part of the invoice data; the app points this out.

The app says “This PDF does not contain an embedded e-invoice”.

The PDF is an ordinary PDF invoice without machine-readable data. You can open such files in any PDF viewer; there is nothing for Rechnungslupe to read.

Does the app change my invoice?

No. Rechnungslupe only reads the file. The original stays unchanged. The PDF you can create from the view is a readable rendering; the original file always takes precedence.

Does the app check whether an invoice is correct?

Rechnungslupe presents the data in a readable form. The app does not provide tax advice and does not assess whether an invoice is legally valid.

How much does the app cost?

Rechnungslupe is free. Additional features are planned as a one-off purchase (“Pro”); everything the app can do today will remain free.
