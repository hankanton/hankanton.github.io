Rechnungslupe: E-Rechnung

Read e-invoices with ease – on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Rechnungslupe displays electronic invoices in the XRechnung, ZUGFeRD and Factur-X formats in a clear layout: seller and buyer, invoice lines, VAT, totals, payment details and attachments. You can save, print and share the view as a PDF.

Offline. Invoices are processed solely on your device. The app makes no network connection to do so, needs no account and contains no advertising, no tracking and no usage analytics. See the privacy policy for details.

Help

How do I open an invoice from Mail?

Tap the attachment (XML or PDF file). In the share sheet, choose either the “Rechnungslupe” app or, further down, the “View in Rechnungslupe” action. The action shows the invoice right inside Mail without switching apps.

How do I open an invoice from the Files app?

Touch and hold the file, then choose “Share” or “Open With” and “Rechnungslupe”. Alternatively, open Rechnungslupe and tap “Open invoice”. On a Mac, use File → Open…, right-click and choose “Open With”, or drag the file onto the app icon.

Which formats are supported?

  • XRechnung in both syntaxes (UBL and UN/CEFACT CII) as an XML file
  • ZUGFeRD 2.x and Factur-X: PDF files with an embedded XML invoice. With the MINIMUM and BASIC WL profiles, the file contains only part of the invoice data; the app points this out.

The app says “This PDF does not contain an embedded e-invoice”.

The PDF is an ordinary PDF invoice without machine-readable data. You can open such files in any PDF viewer; there is nothing for Rechnungslupe to read.

Does the app change my invoice?

No. Rechnungslupe only reads the file. The original stays unchanged. The PDF you can create from the view is a readable rendering; the original file always takes precedence.

Does the app check whether an invoice is correct?

Rechnungslupe presents the data in a readable form. From version 1.1, a plausibility check recalculates the totals, looks for typical details expected by EN 16931 and XRechnung, checks the IBAN and dates and, for ZUGFeRD, compares the key values with the text of the PDF. It describes anomalies in the data; it does not replace the official validation tool. The app does not provide tax advice and does not assess whether an invoice is legally valid. The original file always takes precedence.

How much does the app cost?

Rechnungslupe is free. From version 1.1 there is also “Rechnungslupe Pro”, a one-off in-app purchase with no subscription. It applies on iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same Apple Account and can be shared via Family Sharing. Everything the app can do without Pro remains free.

What does Rechnungslupe Pro offer? (from version 1.1)

  • the list of anomalies from the plausibility check and the check report as a PDF;
  • an archive with search, filters and notes – optionally synced via iCloud to all your devices;
  • the package for your tax adviser: PDF, original and check report in one ZIP file;
  • GiroCode for your banking app, “Copy IBAN” and a reminder of the early payment discount deadline;
  • on the Mac, the readable invoice in the Finder preview (Space bar).

I bought Pro, but it is not unlocked.

Open “About Rechnungslupe” (ⓘ) in the app and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple Account as for the purchase. On the Mac, the readable Finder preview appears after you have opened the app once.

How do the archive and iCloud syncing work? (from version 1.1)

Invoices only go into the archive if you choose “Add to archive” in the invoice view. The archive is kept on your device. You turn on syncing via iCloud in the archive with the cloud symbol; the invoices then appear on all devices with the same Apple Account. The data is kept in your private iCloud storage, which only you can access. When you turn syncing off, you can delete the copies in iCloud; the invoices on the device are kept.
